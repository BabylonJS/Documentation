## Debugging Skeletons and Rigging Issues

When something isn't working right, simple debugging tools can help us identify and fix the issue quickly. Babylon.js 4.2 added an all-new Skeleton Viewer. Built right into [The Inspector](/legacy/inspector), it makes it quick and easy to debug pesky rigging issues in your scene, from rendering bones and joints to visualizing skin weights.
