Welcome to the wonderful 3D world of Babylon.js and 3D on the web. Beginners learning JavaScript should be able to learn a lot from the examples; however, we strongly suggest that you gain a working knowledge of JavaScript before starting to develop your own projects.

As you move through this introduction, you will explore the features of Babylon.js. We present code examples using our Playground, show you how to add models to your website, and explain how to import your own models into your Babylon.js game or app. Once you have covered all the chapters, you will have a foundational awareness of what Babylon.js has to offer. When you are ready, our full range of docs will help you dive deeper into Babylon.js. One day soon, you may find yourself part of the Babylon.js community, creating new ways to work with Babylon.js and, who knows, contributing to it as well.
