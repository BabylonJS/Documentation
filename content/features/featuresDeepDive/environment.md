## Realistic Environments

Of course, when working on games or apps, the level of realism achieved will depend on your design decisions. Babylon.js can help you achieve whatever level you want, and in this section we cover some of the ways to do this. For example, lighting the background, creating lens flares, adding sky and fog, or working with HDR.

Other topics need sections of their own, such as infinite terrains, post processes, and shaders.
