## From One to Many

Now we take a big step as we turn the simple box into a house, or even two. Since houses do not float, we will create some ground and then see how we can position, rotate, and resize the house and place it on the ground. Since a blank box still looks like a blank box, we will add texture to give it the appearance of windows and doors. To keep the rain out, let's add a roof mesh and merge the two meshes together. Of course, a couple of houses do not make a village, so we will see how we can copy a mesh as many times as we wish. Since builders are noisy workers, we will also show you how to include sounds and then silence them immediately, as anything other than the sounds you want to work with can be distracting.
