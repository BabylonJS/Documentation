## Welcome To The Babylon.js Documentation

Hello and welcome to the Babylon.js Documentation. In these pages, you will find everything you could ever want to know about this powerful, beautiful, simple, and completely open-source web rendering engine. Whether you are just starting your Babylon.js journey, or you are a seasoned veteran, it is our sincere hope that the information contained here will help you bring your ideas to life.

We know that each person comes to Babylon.js with their own background, experiences, and learning style. We also understand that each of you has a different goal in mind for your learning journey. We have done our best to organize these doc pages to help as many people as possible achieve their learning goals efficiently. Our documentation has two main parts: one for those who want to dive right into Babylon.js and learn about the engine and its features, and another for those who prefer to start by understanding how to get Babylon.js set up and integrated into a web application. In the coming pages, you'll find paths optimized for each of these learning styles.

However, before you go any further, we strongly encourage (and humbly ask) EVERYONE to [Start Your Babylon.js Journey Here.](journey/theFirstStep)
